Software for arbitrage in betting and Web3

DevUnion builds tools that find price differences between markets and turn them into data you can act on: parsers, APIs and arbitrage software.

How a fork works

1 / k + p < 1

The same outcome is priced differently at a bookmaker and on a prediction market. When 1 / k + p is below one, backing both sides locks in a spread before the result.
