VILKA — self-hosted typefaces
=============================

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(full text: OFL-1.1.txt). The OFL permits use, embedding and
redistribution in commercial work; it forbids selling the fonts on
their own and requires this notice to travel with the files.

Unbounded          display / headings
  by NaN (nan.xyz). Open source and funded by the Polkadot treasury.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Unbounded

Onest              prose / reading text
  by Dmitri Voloshin, Andrey Kudryavtsev.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Onest

Martian Mono       technical text, HUD, ASCII panels
  by Roman Shamin, Evil Martians.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Martian+Mono

Files are the latin and cyrillic subsets of each variable font, taken
from the Google Fonts CDN and served from this repository, so the site
makes no third-party requests.
